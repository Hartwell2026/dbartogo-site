Good to Know

Frequently asked questions.

How much does a mobile bar service cost?

Packages start at $600 for intimate gatherings and scale with guest count, bar style, and cocktail menu. Most weddings and private events range from $1,200 to $3,500. Every quote is custom and includes licensed bartenders, the mobile bar, glassware, garnishes, and breakdown.

Do you provide the alcohol?

We offer flexible plans con o sin licor — with or without alcohol. We can build a fully stocked package, or provide the bartenders, bar, mixers, ice, glassware, and garnishes while you supply the spirits. Either way, your plan is customized to your budget and event.

Are your bartenders licensed and insured?

Yes. Every bartender is certified in responsible alcohol service, and we carry full liquor liability and general liability insurance. We're happy to provide a certificate of insurance for any venue that requires one.

What areas do you serve?

We serve all of South Florida — Miami-Dade, Broward, and Palm Beach counties. Travel beyond a 30-mile radius is available for a small additional fee.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend 4–8 weeks for weddings and large events, though we accommodate last-minute requests when our calendar allows. Peak winter and spring dates book quickly.
